## 2. What we did

### 2.1 Voice breaking after about 1 minute: fixed
- **Cause:** Murf sends several sentences over one connection with their audio chunks mixed together. We played them in arrival order, so sentences broke into each other. The first fix tried (8 kHz / gain change) was the wrong diagnosis.
- **Fix:** `server/murf_tts.py` now plays sentences strictly in the order they were sent, holding back later ones. Test: `scripts/murf_order_test.py`.
- **Proof:** the next calls had no breaks: no clipping, at most one 80 ms gap that's probably just the join between two sentences.

### 2.2 Silent bot, and the Gemini key problem: fixed
- **The free AI Studio tier** returned 503 errors and hung.

### 2.3 Caller ID: fixed

## 3. DSP: what we tested and what we learned

| Option | Result |
|---|---|
| **Our own boost (AGC)** and **RNNoise** | AGC turned "हो" into "नाही". RNNoise cut 52 words to 10. **Off** (`VOICE_DSP=false`). |
| **DeepFilterNet3** | Loses 25–67% of words. At a gentler setting it turned **"नाही" into "होय"**. |
| **WebRTC noise suppression + auto-gain** | **Best of the set.** It recovered real words on the quietest calls and never flipped a "हो". But it **made phrases up from boosted silence** ("अभिनंदन करतो", "Good evening"). |
| **ClearVoice FRCRN** | **Erased the quiet caller completely**, and runs **10.7× slower than real time**. |
| **Competing voice in the background** | **No filter helped.** These tools remove noise, not other people's voices. |
| **Boosting only the voice detector (Silero)** | **Worse:** it heard 14% of the caller's speech untouched and 4% with +12 dB. |

**Conclusion.** The audio on the test calls is clean; the background measured −72 dBFS. Sarvam mishears it even untouched. No filter makes a speech model understand better. **The bottleneck is speech recognition, not noise.**

## 4. Blockers

| # | Blocker | Impact | Way forward |

| **B1** | **Sarvam mishears quiet or unclear callers.** On f28ccb7a nearly every line was garbage, even on untouched audio. | Calls fail before the complaint is even heard | Try **Sarvam's `saaras:v3-realtime`**, which accepts a list of words to listen for ("तक्रार", "पाणी", Pune areas). Untested so far. Chirp 3 was rejected on 14 Oct (lost an address, about 2 s slower). |
| **B2** | **Silero never hears quiet callers** (0% of the caller's speech at the 0.85 threshold) | The 3 s is counted from Sarvam's last transcript, so a long quiet sentence can be cut off | Replay-test `VAD_CONFIDENCE` at 0.7. It hears 14–34% of quiet speech with few false triggers. At 0.5, echo of the bot's own voice would pause it about 7% of the time. |
| **B3** | **Vertex rate limits (429).** A few tests at once hit "resource exhausted", and the fallback model rejects our settings. | A live call that hits a 429 could go **silent** | Add a retry with backoff; fix or replace `GEMINI_FALLBACK_MODEL` (3.5 Flash-Lite rejects `thinking_budget=0`). Never run LLM tests while a call is live. |
| **B4** | **Competing voices** | A noisy household or street keeps confusing it | A paid SDK (Krisp or ai-coustics, about $149–599 a month), or a speaker-isolation model that needs a sample of the caller's voice. Neither is set up. |
| **B5** | **Replies are slower by design** (3 s rule) | About 4–5 s per reply | Accepted trade-off. It could become adaptive later: 3 s only for callers who pause a lot. |
