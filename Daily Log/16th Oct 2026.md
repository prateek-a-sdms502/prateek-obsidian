## 5. The five steps

### Step 1: voice detection (Silero) 0.85 → 0.7
- **New finding:** even a loud, clear caller was only **7%** detected at 0.85. That's why the bot spoke over people.
- **Replay test:** cut-ins on the clear call went 1 → 0, and on the quiet call 9 → 6.
- **False fires:** at most 0.7% in silence, and at most 1.1% while the bot speaks.

### Step 2: speaking up after silence
- **What it does:** after 8 s of silence once the bot stops, it says "मी ऐकतोय, सांगा." Another 8 s brings "हॅलो, तुम्हाला माझा आवाज येतोय का? बोला.", and then nothing more until the caller speaks.
- **Built on** Pipecat's own idle timer.
- **Proof:** `idle_prompt_test` 8/8, and live: both lines came exactly 8.0 s after the bot stopped.

### Step 3: Gemini rate limits
- **Retries:** a 429/500/503 is retried 3 times with backoff inside the Google client.
- **Proof:** the retry is confirmed to cover streaming requests, against a fake endpoint that refused twice and then answered.
- **Thinking setting:** off for 2.5 and 3.1 Flash-Lite; the documented "minimal" for 3.5 and newer.
- **Correction:** the fallback model was **not** broken on Vertex. The earlier error came from AI Studio.
- **Proof:** `gemini_retry_test` 10/10; real-Gemini suite 12/12.

### Step 4: speech-to-text switched to Sarvam real-time `saaras:v4` with a place prompt
- **Followed the vendor-research protocol:** current docs, a short probe, a sustained 4-call test, and a check of every critical behaviour.
- **Why legacy was running:** there was no recorded failure. It was left over from the 14 Oct latency baseline.
- **Results on real calls:**

| Said | Legacy v3 (before) | v4 + prompt (now) |
|---|---|---|
| Baner Road | ❌ भानेरी / भाणे | ✅ बाणेर road |
| Virbhadra Nagar | ≈ Veerabhadranagar | ✅ वीरभदरनगर |
| Amar Techno Park | ❌ "Amar Techno Farms" | ✅ |
| Quiet caller's opening | ❌ "मला चक्रा…" | ✅ "मला तक्रार नोंदवायची आहे" (also live) |

- **Sarvam's strict place list (`keyterms`) is kept OFF.** It forced a wrong listed place: "Virbhadra Nagar" became "Warje Nagar".
- **Two settings moved with the switch:**
  - Sarvam's pause setting went from 600 to 1200 ms; shorter values split one sentence in two.
  - The 3 s turn rule now applies to this model too. It used to drop to 0.1 s.
- **Proof:** `realtime_stt_test`, `realtime_keyterms_test` and `turn_wait_test` all pass.

### Step 5: missing places added
- **Added:** Veerbhadra Nagar, Pyramid Axis and Amar Techno Park (all in Baner), in a hand-kept list (`server/data/local_places.csv`) merged into the place list.
- **Proof:** `local_places_test` 10/10; existing place and address suites unaffected.
